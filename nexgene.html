NexGene

A health-tech prototype exploring predictive approaches to disease prevention using genomic, biomarker, molecular, and lifestyle data.

This is an exploratory product concept and prototype — not a clinical diagnostic system.

The idea

Most health experiences react after something has already gone wrong. NexGene explores what it might look like to design around earlier signals — integrating different layers of personal data into a coherent, understandable experience focused on prevention and precision.

01

Multi-layered signals

Genomic insights, biomarkers, molecular data, and lifestyle inputs treated as parts of one picture.

02

Clarity over complexity

Health data is dense. The design challenge is making it legible and actionable without false certainty.

03

Prevention-oriented

Shifting the experience upstream — toward risk awareness, early signals, and informed decisions.

Status

Where it is

Prototype stage

Currently exploring product concepts, information architecture, and interface directions. Not a live clinical product.
