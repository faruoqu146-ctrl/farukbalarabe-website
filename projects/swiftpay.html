SwiftPay UX Redesign

Reducing drop-off and increasing trust through improvements to onboarding, login, home, and transaction experiences.

Role UI/UX Design
Type Fintech Case Study
Focus Onboarding · Trust · Clarity
Outcome Clearer flows, stronger trust signals

Problem

Many fintech products lose users in the first few sessions. Friction in onboarding, unclear security cues, and dense transaction screens can quietly erode confidence. This redesign focused on the critical early journey: sign-up, login, the home surface, and core money-movement experiences.

Goals

  • Reduce drop-off during onboarding and first login
  • Increase perceived trust and safety without adding noise
  • Make the home experience immediately useful and scannable
  • Clarify transaction states and feedback

Approach

The work prioritized decision-making over decoration. Key changes included:

  • Simplified onboarding steps with clearer progress and expectations
  • Stronger but calmer security and verification cues
  • Rebalanced home hierarchy so balances, recent activity, and primary actions are obvious
  • More explicit transaction feedback and status communication

Every change was tied back to reducing cognitive load and reinforcing that the product is careful with the user’s money and data.

Outcome

The redesigned flows present a clearer path from first open to first successful action. Trust signals are integrated into the experience rather than bolted on as afterthoughts. The case demonstrates how measured UX decisions in high-stakes domains can improve both completion and confidence.
