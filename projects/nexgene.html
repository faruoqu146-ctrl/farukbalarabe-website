NexGene

Exploring predictive approaches to disease prevention through multi-layered health data.

Role Product Design · Concept
Type Health-Tech Prototype
Status Ongoing
Focus Clarity · Prevention · Data UX

Context

Healthcare experiences are often reactive. By the time a person engages with a digital health product, something has usually already gone wrong or become urgent. NexGene is an exploration of a different framing: what if the product experience was designed around earlier signals and prevention?

The prototype draws on the idea of combining genomic information, biomarkers, molecular insights, and lifestyle data into a single, coherent product surface — without claiming clinical diagnostic capability.

Design challenges

  • Making dense, multi-source health data feel legible rather than overwhelming
  • Avoiding false certainty or overclaiming while still being useful
  • Designing for trust in a domain where stakes are high
  • Creating a visual and interaction language that feels intelligent and calm

Approach

The work so far has focused on product framing, information hierarchy, and early interface directions. Emphasis is placed on progressive disclosure, clear status communication, and a restrained visual system that supports focus rather than spectacle.

This remains an active prototype. Deeper case material, flows, and interface explorations will be added as the project progresses.

Note

NexGene is a conceptual and design exploration. It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, or clinical product.
