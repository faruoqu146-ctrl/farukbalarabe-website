I’m Faruk Balarabe Murtala — a medical student and product builder based in Nigeria. I design digital products and interfaces with a focus on clarity, usability, and real-world impact.

My work sits between clinical understanding and product thinking. I’m currently building NexGene, a health-tech prototype exploring data-driven approaches to disease prevention and precision healthcare using genomic, biomarker, molecular, and lifestyle signals.

I also take on select freelance and product-design work — UI/UX, product strategy, prototyping, and design systems — especially for teams that care about thoughtful execution.
